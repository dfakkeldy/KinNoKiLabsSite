# Merchant showcase artwork

The `merchant-shylock-*`, `merchant-bond-*` and `merchant-portia-*` WebP images
are derivatives of existing artwork from Dan Fakkeldy's *Word Is Bond* project,
approved for this website showcase on October 12, 2026. They retain the full
frame; only resolution and encoding changed. `artwork-source.json` records
source and delivered hashes. These are artwork and film stills, not album covers.

This notice grants no additional reuse licence for those images. The website
software's MIT licence and the book editions' CC BY 4.0 cover grant do not extend
to them. No film audio is included or licensed by these image files.

The unchanged `merchant-play-cover.png` and `merchant-novel-cover.png` belong to
the accepted October 4 book editions. Their separate text-and-cover notices are
in the [play package](https://github.com/dfakkeldy/explainer-audiobooks/blob/4bf38dd2b67ffb205344e4331b6dd78fc2668fb1/books/merchant-of-venice-play/LICENSE-CONTENT.md)
and [novel package](https://github.com/dfakkeldy/explainer-audiobooks/blob/4bf38dd2b67ffb205344e4331b6dd78fc2668fb1/books/merchant-of-venice-novel/LICENSE-CONTENT.md).
