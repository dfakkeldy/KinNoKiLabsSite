Where answers come from

The map shows you where.
The records tell you whether.

Use the map and the guide to find a parcel, read its notice and list the questions that matter.

Access, title, boundaries, condition, value and permits are answered by the land registry, the municipality and the right professional: a lawyer, surveyor, planner, inspector or insurer. Confirm live details with the municipality before acting.
