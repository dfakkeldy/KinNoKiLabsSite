Technical problem-solving

When the tools stop lining up, I make the work make sense.

The problem may look like a spreadsheet nobody trusts, a website that no longer fits, repeated data entry, scattered job files, or several tools that do not talk to one another.

I'm Dan Fakkeldy. I trace how the work actually moves, fix the smallest valuable bottleneck first, and leave behind something your team can understand and operate.

Technical Workflow Diagnostic

A fixed engagement around one concrete problem. I trace the people, documents, data, and tools involved, find the costly handoff, and explain the options in plain language.

The recommendation can be no build. Cleanup, a better spreadsheet, or a clearer source of truth may solve the problem before custom software is justified.

Systems Cleanup & Automation

Improve what you already use: organize files, repair a spreadsheet or website, connect forms to the right records, reduce duplicate entry, or automate a repeated handoff.

One visible improvement at a time. The business keeps running, and the people doing the work can review what changed.

Practical Tools & Knowledge Systems

When cleanup is not enough, I can build a focused browser tool, dashboard, searchable company knowledge base, mobile companion, or custom software around the actual job.

The platform follows the work. New software earns its place by solving a defined problem, staying reviewable, and leaving your business in control.

Why it pays

Less friction in the system means more room for the work.

Faster answers — the current file, number, or decision is easier to find and trust.

Fewer missed handoffs — forms, files, due dates, and follow-up stay connected.

Less owner bottleneck — repeated decisions stop living only in one person's memory.

Less duplicate entry — the same information stops being copied between disconnected tools.

Clearer delegation — people can see the expected process and the source behind it.

Less rework — the next task starts from reviewed knowledge instead of a blank page.
