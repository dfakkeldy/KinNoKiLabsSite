What the official source says

Halifax Regional Municipality posted this Request for Proposal (Construction) on 2026-07-24. The title describes street recapitalization, intersection reconfigurations, traffic calming, and sidewalk renewals at various locations. The closing date shown by the procurement portal is 2026-08-10 at 2:00 PM Atlantic time. The official notice page is the authoritative source for scope, documents, and any addenda.

Who might examine this opportunity

Contractors with capacity for municipal road and sidewalk construction work, including traffic management, paving, concrete, and related civil trades. The opportunity may also interest engineering firms supporting bid preparation or estimating.

Questions to verify

  • What are the specific locations and scope quantities in the official documents?
  • Is there a pre-bid meeting or site visit, and is attendance mandatory?
  • What bonding, insurance, and prequalification requirements are stated?
  • What is the evaluation method (price-only, or combined technical and price)?
  • Have any addenda been posted since the initial notice?

Suggested first review steps

  1. Open the official notice and confirm the current closing time and timezone.
  2. Download and read the complete document set, including any addenda.
  3. Confirm bonding, insurance, and safety requirements before investing in estimating.
  4. Check for addenda immediately before the closing date.
