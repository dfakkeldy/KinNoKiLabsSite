Playback & your files

My book won't play / chapters are missing.

Check the files exist locally: in the Files app, long-press your audiobook folder and choose Keep Downloaded so iCloud can't evict the audio. For multi-file books, confirm the files sort correctly by name — you can also drag-reorder in the playlist.

How should I organize my audiobook and EPUB files?

One parent "Audiobooks" folder; inside it, one folder per book named by title; the EPUB or PDF dropped in the same folder as the audio (it auto-imports); zero-padded track numbers (01, 02…). The manual has the full convention, including the iCloud pitfalls.

Can it play my Audible books?

Not while they're DRM-locked. Echo plays open formats only (MP3, M4A, M4B and friends) and does not bypass DRM. Tools like Libation or OpenAudible can export books you own to open formats — see the manual's FAQ for details, including a note on checking the legality in your country.

Reading & alignment

The reader text doesn't match the narration.

Different editions drift. Run Auto-Align Chapters first; for stubborn spots, long-press the paragraph you're hearing and tap Align to Now. Two or three manual anchors usually tame even a messy book.

Auto-alignment is slow or my phone runs warm.

The first run downloads the on-device speech model (~40 MB) and transcription is real work for the Neural Engine. Plug in for the first full-book alignment of a long book; afterwards, incremental repairs are quick.

Apple Watch & study

The watch shows a stale book or position.

Raise the watch and give it a beat — it requests authoritative state from the phone on wake. Keeping both devices on and nearby (Bluetooth/Wi-Fi) helps.

Will flashcards interrupt my listening?

No. Mid-playback flashcard popups are retired: one tap marks a passage without pausing the book, the mark waits in the Card Inbox until you turn it into a card, and reviews happen in Daily Review.

Can I import my Anki decks?

Anki-style JSON decks import today. Direct .apkg import, with scheduling history, is on the roadmap; it is not in Echo 1.0. Details and format notes are in the manual.

What's coming after 1.0?

Brain Dump notes with watch dictation, .apkg import and export, deck JSON export, review by deck, full iCloud study sync, Context Memory place chips and a map view, Chapter Study Mode, gentle hyperfocus reminders, CarPlay, Mac Insights and full Mac reader parity. The roadmap is public.

Your data & privacy

Does Echo track my location?

Context Memory is optional and off by default. With permission, the device can provide a precise fix; Echo rounds coordinates before asking Apple's geocoder for a place name and stores the resulting context locally. Delete Context Memory removes the app's local context records, not providers' records or backups. See the Echo Privacy Policy.

Will my flashcards and bookmarks sync between devices?

This release does not promise complete private synchronization of flashcards, decks, bookmarks or playback position. Files in an iCloud folder follow Apple's file services, and Watch features transfer selected state and voice memos with the paired iPhone. Importing a book may look up matching public alignment anchors when network access is permitted, with local fallback for an ordinary lookup failure. This release does not expose public-alignment uploads.

Does Echo work offline?

Local playback, reading, narration and alignment can work with the required files and models already present. Model downloads, optional community-alignment lookup, website capture, external AI and Audiobookshelf use a network connection. External AI sends book text to the configured provider.

Where is my data? Can I get it out?

Your audio stays where you put it (Echo reads in place). Echo's own data lives in a local database on your device with an open-source schema. Bookmarks and whole-book study-note bundles export to Markdown today, ready for Obsidian, Logseq, or Notion. Portable JSON deck export is on the roadmap. Your data is never hostage.
