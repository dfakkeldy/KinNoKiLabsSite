Echo

For Every Mind — turn listening into learning.

The audiobook player that helps you remember what you heard. Built on a mail route, nights and weekends, for an AuDHD brain that learns in stolen, interrupted minutes.

  • Word-perfect read-along. True karaoke, tuned on-device to the real audio.

  • One-tap flashcards. Spaced repetition (FSRS / FSRS-4.5) with the narrator's audio attached.

  • Smart Rewind. Interruptions stop costing you context.
