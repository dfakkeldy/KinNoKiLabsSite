MacroMark and Turn Timer do not send your notes, templates or recordings to KinNoKi Labs. We do not operate app accounts or note servers, and we do not sell your personal data. We do not use third-party analytics trackers or advertising frameworks.

Which policy applies:

  • MacroMark, Turn Timer and this website: this page. The app descriptions below do not describe Echo or the native NS Marks The Spot app.
  • Echo: the Echo Privacy Policy covers local processing, optional network features, community alignment lookup with public uploads deferred for 1.0, permissions, exports and support handling.
  • NS Marks The Spot (native iPhone and iPad app): its own privacy policy governs that app.

MacroMark and Turn Timer

Apple services provide optional iCloud storage, speech recognition, purchases and related system features. You can also choose to export or share files.

iCloud sync. Some of our apps use Apple’s iCloud to sync your data across your personal devices. This data is stored securely in your private iCloud account. We do not have access to this data, nor can we view or manage it. All iCloud synchronization is handled exclusively by Apple’s services according to their privacy policies.

Speech and location in MacroMark. Speech recognition and dictation may use Apple system services depending on device capability, language and OS behaviour. Optional location macros may use Apple’s geocoding service to turn coordinates into a place name. These services follow Apple’s privacy policy. MacroMark does not upload your audio to KinNoKi Labs.

Support and beta feedback. If you contact us, we receive what you choose to send, such as your email, message, screenshots or logs. Apple’s TestFlight beta program may also share crash reports, submitted feedback, device information, OS version and build information with the developer.

Local storage. Any data generated by our apps that is not synced via iCloud is stored locally on your device. We cannot access this information.

This website

kinnokilabs.com is a static site with no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. Some pages keep small amounts of data in your own browser so they work offline and remember where you were. The dark/light theme and OpenDyslexic font toggles are remembered in your browser too. None of it is sent to us, and clearing this site’s data in your browser removes all of it.

Tools and games

  • Web Tools Preferences stay in browser storage on that device. The EPUB reader keeps the books you import in a browser database on that device only.
  • Games Progress, records, and sound preferences stay in that browser.
  • Gas Price Watch Official prices and experimental estimates are downloaded from a public GitHub data feed, with a saved site snapshot as a fallback. Your zone choice stays in your browser and is not included in those requests. There are no accounts, location requests, submissions or trackers. GitHub and this site’s hosting receive the ordinary connection information associated with serving a public web request.

Listening and film

  • Echo Listening Room and Fiction Listening Room Playback position, speed, and theme stay in that browser. Audio and text stream from public GitHub URLs when you press play.
  • Shakespeare film The Word Is Bond film plays in YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player at youtube-nocookie.com. Nothing from YouTube loads until you press Play. After that, the player is YouTube’s, and Google’s privacy policy covers it. The “Watch on YouTube” link opens youtube.com instead.

Maps

  • Fishing map Your month, species, water and drive-from choices stay in that browser. The map library loads from cdnjs.cloudflare.com and the fonts from Google Fonts, like any web request. Directions open Google Maps or Apple Maps only when you tap them.
  • NS Marks The Spot browser map Maps you import, saved setups, and any position this device reports stay in that browser. Map tiles and layer data are fetched from the public sources named in each layer’s attribution; those requests carry only the ordinary information any web request carries. Tax-sale listings never show owner names.
  • Address and driveway map Its address search, driveway traces, and offline pack stay in that browser only; no resident names. Aerial imagery is not stored offline. Clearing this site’s data removes it.
