## Questions to verify

- What are the specific formats, quantities, and packaging requirements?
- Is there a minimum order guarantee, or is delivery entirely on-demand?
- What delivery location(s) and frequency are specified?
- Are there brand, quality, or compatibility specifications?
- What is the expected duration of the standing offer?

## Suggested first review steps

1. Open the official notice and confirm the current closing time and timezone.
2. Check the specification details — exact media types, packaging, and labelling.
3. Verify that your supply chain can meet the delivery and call-up terms.
4. Review the pricing structure and any volume adjustments.
