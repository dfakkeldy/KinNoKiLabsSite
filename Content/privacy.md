# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 18, 2026

This page describes KinNoKi Labs apps and this website. The native iPhone and iPad app NS Marks The Spot has its own [privacy policy](/nsmarksthespot-privacy/), which governs that app. The remaining app descriptions below do not describe the native NS Marks The Spot app.

Echo's native apps have their own [Echo Privacy Policy](/echo-privacy/). The shared app descriptions below apply to the other named apps; they do not describe Echo's native data handling.

## Data Collection
Our applications MacroMark and Turn Timer do not collect or sell your personal data, and never send it to us — we have no servers. The only data that ever leaves your device goes to Apple's own services (iCloud sync), all described below. We do not use third-party analytics trackers or advertising frameworks.

## iCloud Sync (other apps)
Some of our apps use Apple’s iCloud to sync your data across your personal devices. This data is stored securely in your private iCloud account. We do not have access to this data, nor can we view or manage it. All iCloud synchronization is handled exclusively by Apple's services according to their privacy policies.

## Echo native apps
See the [Echo Privacy Policy](/echo-privacy/) for local processing, optional network features, community alignment lookup with public uploads deferred for 1.0, permissions, exports and support handling.

## Local Storage (other apps)
Any data generated by our apps that is not synced via iCloud is stored locally on your device. We cannot access this information.

## This Website
kinnokilabs.com is a static site with no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. Some pages keep small amounts of data in your own browser so they work offline and remember where you were. None of it is sent to us, and clearing this site's data in your browser removes all of it.

- **Theme and font:** The dark/light theme and OpenDyslexic font toggles are remembered in that browser.
- **Web Tools** (/tools): Preferences stay in browser storage on that device. The EPUB reader keeps the books you import in a browser database on that device only.
- **Gas Price Watch** (/gas): Official prices and experimental estimates are downloaded from a public GitHub data feed, with a saved site snapshot as a fallback. Your zone choice stays in your browser and is not included in those requests. There are no accounts, location requests, submissions or trackers. GitHub and this site's hosting receive the ordinary connection information associated with serving a public web request.
- **Games** (/games): Progress, records, and sound preferences stay in that browser.
- **Shakespeare film** (/shakespeare/merchant-of-venice): The Word Is Bond film plays in YouTube's privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com). Nothing from YouTube loads until you press Play. After that, the player is YouTube's, and Google's privacy policy covers it. The "Watch on YouTube" link opens youtube.com instead.
- **Listening Rooms** (/listen and /fiction): Playback position, speed, and theme stay in that browser. Audio and text stream from public GitHub URLs when you press play.
- **Fishing map** (/fishing): Your month, species, water and drive-from choices stay in that browser. The map library loads from cdnjs.cloudflare.com and the fonts from Google Fonts, like any web request. Directions open Google Maps or Apple Maps only when you tap them.
- **NS Marks The Spot browser map:** Maps you import, saved setups, and any position this device reports stay in that browser. Map tiles and layer data are fetched from the public sources named in each layer's attribution; those requests carry only the ordinary information any web request carries. Tax-sale listings never show owner names. The address and driveway map at /poker keeps address search, driveway traces, and its offline pack in that browser only; no resident names. Aerial imagery is not stored offline. Clearing this site's data removes it.
