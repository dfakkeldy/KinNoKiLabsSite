Turn Timer is a colourful visual countdown you can read from across the room. Start a quick timer, or build a sequence of named rounds for game night, a recipe, a classroom, a meeting or a morning routine, and reuse it whenever you need it.

The browser version runs on any device, with nothing to install, and can be added to your home screen. The iPhone app is a TestFlight beta, not on the App Store yet.

## How it works

<ol class="tt-steps">
<li><strong>Start simply.</strong> Use the quick timer, or pick an editable starter: Game Night, Recipe Steps, Plant Watering, Classroom Stations, Meeting Agenda or Morning Routine.</li>
<li><strong>Build your sequence.</strong> Give each round a name, duration, colour, sound and emoji. Repeat a sequence, pause, skip, restart, or do a turn over.</li>
<li><strong>Keep what you reuse.</strong> Save templates, return to recent sessions, and share portable <code>.turntimer</code> files that import without overwriting your own.</li>
</ol>
