Turn Timer is a colourful visual countdown you can read from across the room. Start a quick timer, or build a sequence of named rounds for game night, a recipe, a classroom, a meeting or a morning routine, and reuse it whenever you need it. The iPhone app is in beta on TestFlight; it is not on the App Store yet.

[Join the Turn Timer beta on TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/s7w4YGWU), or [try it in your browser](/tools/turn-timer/) on any device. The browser version can be installed to your home screen.

## How it works

- **Start simply.** Use the quick timer, or pick an editable starter: Game Night, Recipe Steps, Plant Watering, Classroom Stations, Meeting Agenda or Morning Routine.
- **Build your sequence.** Give each round a name, duration, colour, sound and emoji. Repeat a sequence, pause, skip, restart, or do a turn over.
- **Keep what you reuse.** Save templates, return to recent sessions, and share portable `.turntimer` files that import without overwriting your own.

## Free, with a one-time Pro unlock

The quick timer, the six starters, playback, one custom template and five recent sessions are free. A one-time Turn Timer Pro purchase adds unlimited templates, full history and export, sharing, private iCloud sync and template widgets. No subscription, ads or third-party tracking.
