MacroMark is voice capture for people whose notes live in Markdown. Tap your Watch complication, speak, lower your wrist, and let MacroMark append the thought to today's note.

MacroMark 1.0 is live on the App Store and free. Version 1.1 is in testing.

## A note before the thought cools

MacroMark is built around the moment when opening a phone would be enough friction to lose the idea. The Watch collects, the phone processes, and your vault receives one clean Markdown entry.

1. **Tap from your watch face.** Deep links open instant capture without a hunt through app screens.
2. **Speak, pause, keep going.** The custom audio path is designed for long, messy thoughts instead of tiny dictated snippets.
3. **Lower your wrist to save.** Recordings are queued durably on the Watch and delivered to iPhone when the connection is ready.
4. **Land in today's file.** Processed notes append to a dated, plain-text daily note in your own files on iCloud Drive — where your Markdown vault already lives — instead of scattering across throwaway documents.

## Built for trust

- **Reliability core** Watch captures stay queued until the iPhone has processed them and the export has succeeded or is safely queued for retry.
- **Status and recovery** Export status labels, a needs-attention filter, partial-transcription warnings and retry actions keep capture state visible.
- **Review by date** Check today's, yesterday's or any earlier daily note from the phone inbox or the Watch daily log.
- **App Intents** Instant capture, typed capture, daily-log review and append-text actions are available in Shortcuts and Siri.
- **Setup proof** Settings show where notes go and can write a test note before you rely on Watch capture.
- **Daily-note shape** Timestamp style, separators and an optional append heading are yours to configure.

## Privacy stays plain

MacroMark is built around local capture, Apple speech services where applicable, and user-owned note files that stay useful outside the app. It is not a social inbox or analytics product.
