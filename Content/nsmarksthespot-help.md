# NS Marks The Spot help

Questions or problems with NS Marks The Spot? Here's where to find the maps and how to reach me.

## Where to find the maps

- [Full browser map](/apps/nsmarksthespot/map/) — the whole research map, in your browser
- [Rhodena Wind map](/rhodena), the focused map at `/rhodena` — the same map with a Rhodena focus
- [Fishing map](/fishmap), at `/fishmap` — the same map with a fishing focus
- [Strait and Bras d'Or Fishing Map](/fishing/), the static guide, separate from the research map

## Contact

Hit a bug, or have a feature request?

- **Email Support:** [hello@kinnokilabs.com](mailto:hello@kinnokilabs.com)
- **Issue Tracker:** [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/dfakkeldy/ns-marks-the-spot/issues)

I try to respond within a day or two. What the iPhone app stores, and what it sends, is in the [NS Marks The Spot privacy policy](/nsmarksthespot-privacy/).
