Interrupter watch

The watch compares estimated supply-cost gaps before taxes with a six-cent reference. It uses only real daily quotes. The Board compares different market assessments with its benchmark and has discretion, so this signal does not confirm an official trigger.

Board guidance describes a six-cent change generally sustained over two days, with scope to act on a very large one-day move.

The formula

Wholesale = ROUND(benchmark + forward + clean fuel + wholesale margin
+ transport + carbon charge + federal tax + provincial tax, 2)
Pump price = ROUND((wholesale + retail margin + retail adjustment) × (1 + HST), 1)

These equations return cents per litre. Divide by 100 for dollars. The minimum and maximum use their respective retail margins.

The official benchmark uses 90% New York Harbor CBOB and 10% Chicago ethanol daily spot assessments. Each day's blended high/low average is converted with its Bank of Canada USD/CAD rate, then the daily results are averaged.

The automatic estimate uses changes in Halifax E10 rack prices to predict the combined benchmark, forward correction and clean-fuel subtotal. It applies known taxes and margins and carries the last retail adjustment. Rack prices are already in Canadian cents per litre. The earlier EIA conventional-gasoline estimate was withdrawn; its data and results are preserved for comparison.

Commercial assessments and the Board's next forward correction are needed for an exact advance calculation. The clean-fuel adjustor currently uses Argus Canada CFR compliance costs. The Python calculator also accepts a CSV of daily CBOB and ethanol assessments.
